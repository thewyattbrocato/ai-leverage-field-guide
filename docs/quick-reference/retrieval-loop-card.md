# Quick Reference Card — Retrieval Loop (Learning)

**Print this. Keep at your desk. Use for every book/skill.**

---

## The Loop

```
1. METALARN — before you study, map the skill
   What does "good" look like? What are the sub-skills?

2. RETRIEVE — close the source, write/quiz cold
   Ask the model to quiz you, or write what you remember

3. FEEDBACK — compare to source, error = lesson
   The error IS the lesson, not a failure

4. SPACE — wait, then repeat
   1 day → 3 days → 1 week

5. INTERLEAVE — mix topics, don't block
   Shuffle the quiz order

6. CONSOLIDATE — summarize in own words, sleep
   The summary IS the learning artifact
```

**The key is step 2.** Most people reread (feels productive, barely moves needle). Retrieval is uncomfortable because it IS the rep that builds the muscle.

---

## Copy-Paste Template

```
CONTEXT: I'm learning [skill or topic]. Here is what I've read so far (or paste your notes).
TASK: Quiz me. Ask 5 questions that force me to retrieve from memory, not recognize.
  - 3 recall questions (define it / explain it / show the steps)
  - 2 applied questions (here is a scenario, what do you do?)
After I answer, grade me, point to the exact gap, and give one retrieval prompt for tomorrow.
CONSTRAINTS: Don't tell me the answer up front. Make me produce it.
```

## The Schedule

| Session | When | What | Time |
|---------|------|------|------|
| **Metalearn** | Before you start | Map skill, define "good", list sub-skills | 10 min |
| **Session 1** | Day 0 | Retrieve + Feedback | 15 min |
| **Session 2** | Day 1 | Retrieve | 10 min |
| **Session 3** | Day 3 | Retrieve | 10 min |
| **Session 4** | Day 7 | Retrieve + Consolidate | 15 min |

**Total: ~60 minutes over 1 week per book/skill.**

## Why This Works (The Science)

| Principle | Source | What It Means |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **Retrieval > Rereading** | Make It Stick (Brown/Roediger/McDaniel) | Testing yourself beats rereading |
| **Spacing** | How We Learn (Dehaene) | 1 day → 3 days → 1 week beats cramming |
| **Interleaving** | How Learning Happens (Kirschner/Hendrick) | Mix topics, don't block one |
| **Feedback** | Peak (Ericsson) | Error is the lesson; immediate feedback builds mental representations |
| **Consolidation** | How We Learn (Dehaene) | Sleep + spacing = long-term retention |

---

## The Model's Role

| What the Model Does | What YOU Do |
|---------------------|-------------|
| Generates quiz questions | Answers cold (no peeking) |
| Grades your answers | Compares to source |
| Points to exact gap | Learns from the error |
| Generates tomorrow's prompt | Schedules next session |

**You decide what's worth learning. The model forces the retrieval.**

---

## The Discipline

> Retrieval is uncomfortable precisely because it is working. The model's job is to force the retrieval and to grade it honestly. Match the loop to the stakes: a compliance cert gets spaced retrieval; a podcast you'll forget by Friday does not.
